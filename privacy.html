Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

JARVIS News Autopilot is a locally operated desktop application for the owner's Public Mind Report - World News YouTube channel. This public website is informational and has no login, account-registration form, external analytics scripts or advertising trackers.

1. Operator and contact

The YouTube channel owner operates the application for their own newsroom workflow. For questions, open an issue in the public information repository. Do not include passwords or tokens in public messages.

2. Google and YouTube data used

When the owner uses Google's official OAuth sign-in, the desktop app can access YouTube channel identifiers and details, video metadata and upload status, authorized channel-management functions, and read-only YouTube Analytics metrics including views, watch time and engagement. Google OAuth authorization credentials allow subsequent authorized API access. The app never asks for or stores the owner's Google password.

3. Purposes

Data is used to identify the correct YouTube channel, manage authorized video uploads, verify publication states, avoid duplicate uploads, analyze performance and respect owner-controlled review or publishing stages. Data is not sold or used to advertise to third parties.

4. Local storage and security

The app operates on the owner's Windows PC. Its OAuth refresh token is stored locally, protected using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI). Local records may contain source references, video metadata, analytics, job history and logs. This public GitHub Pages website does not collect or store YouTube account information or OAuth tokens. Do not upload secrets or local databases to this public repository.

5. Disclosure to third parties

Necessary data is exchanged with the Google and YouTube APIs to perform functions the owner authorizes. JARVIS also accesses public news and media sources for research, separate from Google OAuth data. Google's processing is described in Google's privacy policy.

6. Retention, control and deletion

Operational records are retained locally as needed for application operations and audit history. The owner can revoke access through Google account permissions and disconnect locally with python jarvis.py youtube disconnect. Local files and logs may need separate deletion by the owner; disconnecting does not automatically erase all historical records.

7. Policy updates

Changes to actual data handling will be reflected here with a revised update date.
