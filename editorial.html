EDITORIAL TRANSPARENCY

How this newsroom works

A useful news site must be clear about what was verified, what was automated and which sources deserve credit.

Automated headline desk

Our homepage gathers short headlines, publication dates and links from established public-information RSS sources, including United Nations News, the Federal Reserve, the Bank of Canada and the U.S. Energy Information Administration. Automated collection does not mean our team independently verified each headline or the underlying event. Read original reporting for context, qualifications and corrections.

Attribution and copyright

The source name and a link to the original article appear with every headline. The feed does not mirror source articles, show source photographs or republish their extended summaries. Publishers retain their rights. The original analysis in our explainers is independently authored for this website.

Dates and freshness

The “feed checked” timestamp identifies when our automated feed last fetched updates, not when any individual event took place. Publication dates are provided by source feeds. We filter obviously stale and future-dated items, but source availability, delayed updates and timestamp errors are possible. Updates are scheduled several times a day, subject to GitHub Actions availability.

Accuracy, conflicts and corrections

Original content should separate verifiable facts, analysis and opinion. When sources disagree, identify the disagreement instead of portraying claims as settled. We do not publish graphic footage as spectacle or treat a social-media post as sufficient proof. To raise a public correction request, use the site issue tracker and include a link to the relevant claim. Do not post confidential information.

Financial independence

The site currently displays no ads, sponsors or affiliate placements. If financial support is introduced later, paid placements will be clearly marked. Sponsors will not control editorial conclusions, and sponsored material will be distinguishable from editorial material.

App versus website

JARVIS News Autopilot is a private Windows application operated by the channel owner for production and authorized YouTube workflows. The public website is a news-and-information page; it is not a place to sign in to JARVIS or submit Google credentials. Read the OAuth app information page and privacy policy.
