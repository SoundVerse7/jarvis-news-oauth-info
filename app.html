What it does

A local, accountable newsroom workflow

This website documents the application and its Google API permissions. It is not a public sign-in portal.

01 / DISCOVER

Research and verify

Review public news sources, record source links and distinguish the event date from the date of reporting.

02 / PRODUCE

Create news videos

Prepare contextual graphics, rights-cleared footage, captions and original audio for YouTube videos and Shorts.

03 / REVIEW

Publish with permission

Connect through Google's OAuth sign-in and respect the owner's upload and publication approvals.

Google account access

Official Google sign-in. No passwords shared.

Only the channel owner authorizes JARVIS. Google handles sign-in directly; the app does not ask for the owner's Google password.

The desktop software is intended for its owner, not public user registration.

What permissions are used?

YouTube: owner-authorized channel and video management, including uploads.

YouTube Analytics: read-only access to channel performance such as views and watch time.

Access can be revoked through Google account permissions.

Transparency

Your channel remains yours.
