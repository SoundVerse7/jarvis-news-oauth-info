Terms of use

Last updated: October 27, 2026

JARVIS News Autopilot is a private, owner-operated Windows desktop tool, not a public SaaS service or an open sign-up platform.

1. Intended use

The app supports news research, source records, video planning and local rendering, and approved YouTube channel activities for Public Mind Report - World News.

2. Authorization

Only the channel owner should authorize the app through Google's official OAuth sign-in. The owner may revoke consent at any time. Connecting an account does not automatically authorize unlimited public publishing.

3. Content and platform rules

Content must respect applicable laws, copyrights, licenses, Google and YouTube policies, and the owner's editorial and approval settings. Archival footage should be clearly labeled and factual claims should be supported by appropriate sources.

4. Publishing controls

Development, controlled-trial and autopilot stages impose separate owner-approval gates. API upload quotas, compliance reviews and availability limits continue to apply even if the site is published.

5. Third parties

Google, YouTube, and third-party news and media providers have independent policies. JARVIS News Autopilot is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Google or YouTube.

6. No access guarantees

This information site does not guarantee OAuth verification, unlimited API requests, public uploads or uninterrupted access. The owner remains responsible for account security, publishing decisions and data controls.

7. Contact and privacy

Read the privacy policy. For inquiries, use the public information repository without posting credentials.
